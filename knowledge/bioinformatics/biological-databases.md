# Key biological databases

- NCBI: GenBank and RefSeq (sequences), SRA (raw sequencing reads), Gene, dbSNP (variants), ClinVar (clinically interpreted variants), GEO (expression data), PubMed (literature), BLAST web service.
- ENA (EMBL-EBI) and DDBJ: partners with GenBank in the International Nucleotide Sequence Database Collaboration (INSDC).
- UniProt: protein sequences and functional annotation (Swiss-Prot is manually reviewed, TrEMBL is automatically annotated).
- Protein Data Bank (RCSB PDB, PDBe, PDBj): experimentally determined 3D structures.
- AlphaFold Protein Structure Database: predicted structures for a large fraction of known proteins, with per-residue confidence (pLDDT).
- Ensembl and UCSC Genome Browser: genome assemblies, gene models and comparative genomics.
- KEGG and Reactome: pathways; Gene Ontology (GO): standardised gene function terms.
- Pfam (now hosted within InterPro) and InterPro: protein families and domains.
- PubChem and ChEMBL: small molecules and bioactivity data, useful for docking studies.
- STRING: protein–protein interaction networks.
Always record database versions or access dates for reproducibility.
