# Standards and regulation for biomedical devices

## Core international standards
- IEC 60601-1: basic safety and essential performance of medical electrical equipment (leakage currents, insulation, means of patient protection).
- IEC 60601-1-2: electromagnetic compatibility (EMC).
- IEC 60601-1-11: equipment used in the home healthcare environment.
- Particular standards, e.g. IEC 60601-2-47 (ambulatory ECG systems) and ISO 80601-2-61 (pulse oximeter equipment).
- ISO 14971: risk management for medical devices.
- ISO 13485: quality management systems for medical device manufacturers.
- IEC 62304: medical device software life-cycle processes.
- IEC 62366-1: usability engineering.
- ISO 10993 series: biological evaluation (biocompatibility) of materials in contact with the body.
- IEC 62133: safety of portable sealed secondary lithium cells and batteries.

## Regulation in India
Medical devices are regulated by the Central Drugs Standard Control Organisation (CDSCO) under the Medical Devices Rules, 2017, using risk classes A (low) to D (high). Clinical investigations need ethics committee approval and follow ICMR guidelines.

## Practical guidance for student prototypes
Treat early prototypes as research tools: battery power only, no connection to mains while worn, documented risk analysis, ethics approval before any human testing, and clear labelling that the device is not for diagnosis.
