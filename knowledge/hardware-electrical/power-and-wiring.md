# Electrical power and wiring basics

## Supply in India
Low-voltage distribution is 230 V single-phase and 400–415 V three-phase (line-to-line) at 50 Hz.

## Power relationships
- Single-phase: P = V·I·cosφ.
- Balanced three-phase: P = √3·VL·IL·cosφ; apparent power S = √3·VL·IL; reactive power Q = √(S² − P²).
- Power factor correction capacitor: Qc = P·(tanφ1 − tanφ2).

## Cable sizing
Select conductors by current-carrying capacity (with derating for grouping, ambient temperature and installation method), voltage drop and short-circuit withstand. Resistivity at 20 °C: copper ≈ 1.72 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m, aluminium ≈ 2.82 × 10⁻⁸ Ω·m. Common voltage-drop design targets: about 3% for lighting and 5% for other loads.

## Protection and safety
- MCB trip curves: B (3–5 × In, resistive loads), C (5–10 × In, general and motor loads), D (10–20 × In, high inrush).
- RCCB/RCD with 30 mA rated residual current for protection against electric shock.
- Proper earthing per IS 3043; wiring practice per IS 732 and the National Electrical Code of India; IEC 60364 internationally.

## Machines
Motor efficiency classes IE1–IE5 (IEC 60034-30-1 and later editions). Induction motor starting currents can be 5–8 × rated current with direct-on-line starting; star-delta starters or VFDs reduce it.

## Important
Mains installations must be carried out and certified by licensed electricians and engineers according to local regulations.
