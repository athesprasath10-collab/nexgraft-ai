# Searching the medical literature

## Sources
- PubMed / MEDLINE (US National Library of Medicine): the core biomedical citation database, indexed with MeSH terms.
- Europe PMC: includes PubMed records, full-text open-access articles (PMC) and preprints; has a free REST API.
- Cochrane Library: systematic reviews of healthcare interventions.
- ClinicalTrials.gov, the WHO International Clinical Trials Registry Platform (ICTRP) and the Clinical Trials Registry – India (CTRI): registered trials, including unpublished ones.
- Embase, Scopus and Web of Science: broader subscription databases.

## Building a search
Identify concepts from the PICO question, list synonyms and MeSH terms for each, combine synonyms with OR and concepts with AND, and use filters (date, study type, humans) carefully. Example: (wearable OR smartwatch) AND (electrocardiogram OR ECG) AND (atrial fibrillation).

## Appraising results
Check study design, sample size, population, comparator, outcome definitions, follow-up, funding and conflicts of interest. Prefer recent systematic reviews for an overview, then read primary studies for details. Preprints are not peer reviewed.
